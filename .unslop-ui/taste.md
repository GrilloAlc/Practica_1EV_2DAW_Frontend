## Bans

- Uppercase text, not only eyebrows (en CataHome no hay etiquetas en mayúsculas)
- Exclamation marks
- Arrows after the text of links and buttons, also "→" and arrow icons
- Numbers used as decoration when they are not a real sequence
- The word "viviendas" in the client-facing pages: CataHome says "casas"
- The word "tickets" in the client-facing pages: CataHome says "consultas" (the agent and admin pages keep "tickets")
